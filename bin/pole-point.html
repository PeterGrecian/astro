Sound
waiting for the first solve
One solve per frame, about every 12 s. Each sound follows the size of its error, so a move the wrong way sounds higher or faster. 0° is 100 Hz at 40 bpm; 5° is 500 Hz at 180 bpm. If the solve is more than 40 s old you hear two low clicks every 4 s instead.
